FAQs

FAQs

Where do I pick up my rental car in Jersey City?

Our vehicles are picked up from our downtown Jersey City location near Exchange Place. Exact pickup instructions are provided before your rental begins.

Is pickup contactless?

Yes. We offer self-service pickup and return, so you can access your vehicle without waiting at a rental counter.

How close are you to Manhattan?

Our pickup area is about a 5-minute walk from Exchange Place PATH. The PATH ride to World Trade Center takes roughly 10 minutes.

How many miles are included?

Our standard rentals include 200 miles per rental day unless otherwise stated in the booking.

Can I extend my rental?

Yes, extensions are available when the vehicle is not already reserved by another guest. Request an extension before your scheduled return time.

Do you offer weekly or longer-term car rentals?

Yes. Weekly and extended rentals are available depending on vehicle availability.
